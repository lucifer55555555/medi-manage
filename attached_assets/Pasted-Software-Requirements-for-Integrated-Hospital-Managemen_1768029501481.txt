Software Requirements for Integrated Hospital Management Solution

(OPD Queuing, Bed Availability, Patient Admission & Inventory Management)

1. General System Requirements
1.1 Core Objectives

The software shall:

Improve OPD efficiency using queuing models

Provide real-time bed availability and admission management

Enable accurate dispensation and inventory management of medicines and consumables

Support integration with NIC-developed modules

Be scalable from hospital-level to city-wide deployment

2. Functional Requirements
2.1 OPD Queuing & Appointment Management
2.1.1 Patient Registration

The system shall support online and on-site OPD registration

The system shall generate unique patient IDs

The system shall store patient demographics and visit history

The system shall support walk-in and appointment-based patients

2.1.2 Queue Management

The system shall implement queuing models (e.g., M/M/1, M/M/c)

The system shall dynamically allocate tokens based on:

Doctor availability

Department workload

Average consultation time

The system shall display real-time queue position

The system shall allow priority handling (emergency, senior citizens, pregnant women)

2.1.3 OPD Dashboards

The system shall provide dashboards for:

Doctors

OPD staff

Hospital administrators

The system shall show:

Waiting time estimates

Number of patients per department

Doctor availability

2.2 Bed Availability Management
2.2.1 Bed Tracking

The system shall maintain real-time bed status:

Available

Occupied

Reserved

Under maintenance

The system shall classify beds by:

ICU

General ward

Emergency

Specialty wards

2.2.2 Bed Allocation

The system shall support automated bed allocation

The system shall prioritize beds for:

Emergency cases

Critical patients

The system shall allow manual override by authorized staff

2.2.3 Bed Transfer & Discharge

The system shall handle:

Inter-ward transfers

Temporary bed blocking

Discharge updates

The system shall automatically update bed availability after discharge

2.3 Patient Admission Management
2.3.1 Admission Workflow

The system shall support paperless admission

The system shall link admission with:

OPD consultation

Emergency referrals

The system shall record admission details:

Diagnosis

Department

Assigned doctor

Bed number

2.3.2 Emergency Admission

The system shall support fast-track emergency admissions

The system shall allow provisional admissions with minimal data

The system shall update full details later

2.4 Medicine Dispensation & Pharmacy Management
2.4.1 Medicine Dispensation

The system shall record prescription-based medicine issuance

The system shall link prescriptions to patient records

The system shall track:

Quantity issued

Date and time

Issuing pharmacist

2.4.2 Pharmacy Operations

The system shall manage:

Hospital pharmacy

Departmental stores

The system shall support batch-wise medicine tracking

2.5 Inventory & Consumables Management
2.5.1 Stock Management

The system shall track inventory for:

Medicines

Medical consumables

Surgical items

The system shall maintain:

Current stock levels

Reorder levels

Supplier details

2.5.2 Expiry & Waste Control

The system shall track expiry dates

The system shall generate alerts for:

Near-expiry items

Expired stock

The system shall support stock disposal records

2.5.3 Procurement & Reorder

The system shall generate auto-reorder alerts

The system shall support demand forecasting using consumption trends

The system shall generate procurement reports

2.6 Integration with NIC & City-Wide Systems
2.6.1 NIC Integration

The system shall integrate with NIC-developed healthcare modules

The system shall support API-based data exchange

The system shall follow government interoperability standards

2.6.2 City-Wide Integration

The system shall support aggregation of hospital-level data

The system shall allow centralized monitoring at:

District level

City level

The system shall support future expansion to state/national platforms

3. Non-Functional Requirements
3.1 Performance Requirements

The system shall support high concurrent users

OPD token generation shall occur in real-time

Dashboards shall refresh data within acceptable latency

3.2 Scalability

The system shall support:

Single hospital deployment

Multi-hospital city-wide deployment

The architecture shall be modular and scalable

3.3 Security Requirements

The system shall enforce role-based access control

The system shall ensure data encryption (at rest and in transit)

The system shall maintain audit logs for all critical actions

The system shall comply with government data security norms

3.4 Reliability & Availability

The system shall ensure high availability

The system shall support backup and disaster recovery

The system shall handle system failures gracefully

3.5 Usability Requirements

The system shall have a user-friendly interface

The system shall support multilingual access

The system shall be usable by:

Doctors

Nurses

Administrative staff

Pharmacists

3.6 Maintainability

The system shall support:

Easy updates

Modular upgrades

The system shall allow configuration without code changes

4. Reporting & Analytics Requirements

OPD waiting time analysis

Bed occupancy trends

Admission and discharge statistics

Medicine consumption reports

Inventory wastage reports

Administrative performance dashboards

5. Compliance & Standards

The system shall comply with:

Government e-Governance guidelines

NIC standards

Data privacy regulations

The system shall support audit and compliance reporting

6. Future Enhancement Readiness

Telemedicine integration

AI-based demand forecasting

Mobile app integration

Public-facing patient portals
